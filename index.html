02 / COMFORT & CONTACT

Fit the sensors.
Find your resting position.

Stable contact and a quiet environment make the recording easier to interpret.

MOND / EAR CLIP

One comfortable clip.

Attach to the earlobe. Support the cable so it does not pull. Keep the same side across repeated tests.

SHIMMER GSR+ / FINGERS

Two secure contacts.

Place the supplied electrodes on the index and middle fingers of your non-dominant hand. Straps should be snug, not tight.

Off the charging dock.Use the Shimmer on battery while the electrodes are attached. Follow the electrode manufacturer's instructions for skin preparation and gel.
Why does contact matter?

Finger movement and changing electrode pressure can change conductance. Record any adjustment in the session notes. GSR changes alone do not identify a specific emotion.

A

In the MOND Windows app

  1. Before connecting, open Settings → Interval acceptance. Set the percentage to 25% and the minimum allowance to 100 ms.
  2. Turn off automatic audio feedback or coaching for this resting test.
  3. Open Collect Data → Scan, select your MOND device, then Connect and Plot. Check that fresh RR values arrive.
  4. Click Stream to LSL. Keep this app and its collection window open. Use only one MOND publisher.
B

In iMotions

Preferences › Global Settings › Sensors › Lab Streaming Layer

  1. Click Search and select MOND_HRV.
  2. Enable data collection and Use LSL timestamp in analysis and export. Save with OK.
  3. In the study's Sensors tab, enable Lab Streaming Layer and your Shimmer GSR+ unit. Use 128 Hz for GSR, as in the established setup.

Stream identity, quality audit & missing values

The primary source ID is mond_hrv_MOND_HRV, with three float32 channels and an irregular rate. It need not appear as a continuous high-frequency signal.

If your configuration supports another inlet, add MOND_HRV_QUALITY to retain raw RR, corrected RR, quality code, divisor, event index and source elapsed time. Otherwise preserve the MOND source audit files.

Missing RR values are rejected observations, not zero intervals. 25% is our selected exploratory timing allowance, not a universal optimum. Host timing does not verify that every device beat was observed.

This timer does not start or stop iMotions, connect to sensors, or create scientific event markers. There is no sound cue.

For the real recording

  1. Confirm live GSR, RR and HR in Sensor Preview. Rolling RMSSD needs enough valid history; an initial gap is expected.
  2. In iMotions, start the actual respondent recording for E1.
  3. Allow the five-minute warm-up and five-minute rest to finish automatically. Avoid talking, touching the sensors or using the keyboard.
  4. Check that iMotions has saved the recording. Then stop and save MOND, and stop its LSL stream.

    YOUR LOCAL COLLECTION LOG

    Take your notes with the data.

    Downloads contain your labels, protocol, checklist and notes. They do not contain sensor recordings.

    Before calculating HRV or comparing with GSR

    Use the same REST_300s window for both streams, with checked clock mapping. Do not take the first 300 seconds from each CSV or directly merge unrelated Timestamp columns. Browser note times are not a replacement for native markers.

    Calculate resting HRV from RR events within the REST phase. The app's whole-session summary includes warm-up, and a mean rolling RMSSD is not the same as whole-phase RMSSD. Preserve missing-event gaps and report retention. MOND's host observations do not independently establish a complete beat series.

    GSR and HRV measure different processes. A single five-minute baseline does not establish device equivalence or a diagnosis.
